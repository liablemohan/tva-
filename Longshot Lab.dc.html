Station 01 / Capture

Preserve what exists.

Temples, monuments, sacred architecture, cultural artefacts, physical spaces. A scanned structure moves from the real thing to a cloud of points, to a digital model, back to a physical replica.

Station 02 / Imagine

Begin with what does not yet exist.

Ideas, sketches, concepts, product design, experiments. The object here has not settled — geometry shifts between several possible forms. You are looking at something before it has decided what it is.

Station 03 / Prototype

Make. Test. Change. Repeat.

Product development, hardware, functional prototypes, iteration, engineering. A partially assembled object moves between concept, CAD, prototype and revision — each pass separates its parts and puts them back a little truer.

Station 04 / Make

Bring it into existence.

3D printing, digital fabrication, custom manufacturing, production, physical objects. The object emerges from layers — one deposited line at a time, a form that was only data a moment ago becomes something you could hold.
